Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how KD-ToolsClub handles information when you visit or use our website and tools.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

KD-ToolsClub ("we", "us", "our", or "KD-ToolsClub") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, free tools, contact forms, and digital products.

By using KD-ToolsClub, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when you contact us or otherwise interact with the website.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Information included in a message you send us
  • Other information you voluntarily submit through our forms

We use this information primarily to respond to your questions, requests, or support inquiries.

3. Text and Content Entered Into Our Tools

Some KD-ToolsClub tools allow you to enter or paste content into your browser.

Our current free tools are designed to process this information directly in your browser. We do not intentionally store the text you enter into these tools on our servers.

However, you should avoid entering confidential, private, sensitive, or personally identifiable information into any online tool unless you are comfortable doing so.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

KD-ToolsClub may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage to remember certain preferences or website interactions.

For example, the website may remember that you have already seen or dismissed a donation message so that it does not repeatedly appear during future visits.

Browser storage is controlled by your browser and can generally be cleared through your browser settings.

5. Analytics and Third-Party Services

KD-ToolsClub may use third-party services in the future for analytics, forms, payments, hosting, advertising, or other website functionality.

These third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party service you use through our website.

6. Contact Forms

If you contact us through a third-party form service, the information you submit may be processed by that service on our behalf.

We may use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, provide support, or communicate with you about your request.

7. Payments and Digital Products

KD-ToolsClub may offer paid digital products and kits.

If payments are processed through a third-party payment provider, payment information may be collected and processed directly by that provider. KD-ToolsClub does not intend to store complete payment card information on its own servers.

Payment providers may have their own privacy policies and terms. You should review those policies before completing a purchase.

8. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to messages and inquiries
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Process purchases through applicable third-party services
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse of the website

9. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, process payments, provide forms, host the website, or deliver other services requested by you.

We may also disclose information when required to comply with applicable law or valid legal requests.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

KD-ToolsClub is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

12. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those external websites.

13. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional personal information. You may also clear browser cookies or local storage through your browser settings.

If you have contacted us and would like to ask about information associated with your communication, you may contact us using our Contact page.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, or applicable requirements.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
